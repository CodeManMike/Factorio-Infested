# The Ichor Engine
## Art Direction and Animation Bible — Consolidated Version 0.2

## Visual Identity

The factory is alive, industrial, hungry, useful, unstable, and capable of becoming hostile. Gross is good. Unclear is bad.

## Visual Pillars

1. Biological industry, not organic decoration.
2. Factorio readability first.
3. Living things must move.
4. State must be visible.
5. Feral looks formerly yours.
6. Sterile tech is return to humanity.
7. Old colony looks human but violated.
8. Bosses are ecosystems.

## Palette Rules

Controlled biology uses deep reds, maroons, wet browns, bone beige, subtle neural glow. Feral uses black ichor, dark red, bruised purple, sickly yellow-green, jagged bone. Sterile uses off-white, grey, steel, blue/green indicators, black seals. Old colony uses rust, concrete, faded yellow, emergency red, infected purple/red.

## State Visual System

| State | Visual Requirement |
|---|---|
| Controlled Idle | slow breathing, symmetrical, stable glow |
| Controlled Working | faster pulse, material flow, tendril activity |
| Starved | pale, sagging, weak glow, slow motion |
| Overheated | swollen red, steam, blistering, rapid pulse |
| Frozen | rigid, blue-grey, cracked, minimal motion |
| Mutating | bulges, asymmetry, twitch bursts, flickering glow |
| Low Loyalty | erratic movement, dark veins, broken rhythm |
| Feral | spikes, teeth, black ichor, aggressive twitching |
| Dead/Necrotic | collapsed grey tissue, leaking dark fluid |
| Sterilized | pale ash, chemical residue, burned-back tissue |

## Substrate Art

Young Substrate is thin film; Mature is dense tissue; Dense is layered arteries; Neural is dark with luminous filaments; Feral is jagged ruptured tissue; Contaminated has lesions; Sterile Ground is clean plating.

## Logistics Art

Tendon Tracks are crude tendon strips; Muscle Ducts show peristalsis; Arterial Ducts are thicker and chitin-railed; Neural Ducts have luminous lines; Sterile Conveyors are sealed clean belts.

Tendrils animate curl → extend → grasp → retract → release.

## Sterile Art

Sterile buildings are clean, sealed, geometric, and calm. Compromised state shows warning lights, stains, seal breaches, and biological intrusion.

## Old Colony Art

Use rusted steel, cracked concrete, broken conveyors, warning markings, dead labs, sealed clean rooms, emergency lights, tissue in vents, and machinery fused with organs.

## Enemy and Boss Art

Native fauna must visibly communicate useful traits: acid sacs, shell plates, wings, burrow claws, electric organs, frost vents, heat sacs, venom glands, sensory organs, radiation lesions.

Alphas are ecosystem rulers: Shellmother, Acid Matriarch, Sky Brood Apex, Burrow Crown, Ember Maw, Frost Warden, Storm Leviathan, Glowback Sovereign.

## Icons and UI Art

Icons must be readable at 32/64px, centered, high contrast, family-coded, no text.

Science icon concepts: Biomass sample, Neural ganglion, Genome sample, Mutation gel, Alpha organ, Sterile cartridge, Cure dose, Severance cut link, Purge charge, Escape package.

## Asset Priority

MVP art: Living/Feral Substrate, Hive Core, Substrate Node, Neural Pylon I, Growth Chamber I, Nutrient Vat I, Digestive Smelter I, Tendon Track, Basic Tendril, Crude Vein, Spike Spitter, Chitin Wall, first feral variants, core icons, alerts.

## Legal Distinctness

Avoid direct StarCraft/Zerg expression: no copied silhouettes, creep tumor look, Overmind/Hydralisk/Overlord clones, or franchise terminology.

## Final Art Identity

> Every machine breathes. Every pipe has a pulse. Every train has hunger. Every lab dreams. Every old building remembers. And when control fails, the factory grows teeth.
