# The Ichor Engine
## Story and Campaign Bible — Consolidated Version 0.2

## Story Premise

The player crash-lands, is saved by a wounded alien hive, grows that hive into living industry, discovers a previous human colony that failed the same experiment, secretly rebuilds sterile industry, manufactures a cure, severs the bond, survives the factory civil war, and launches an escape vessel.

## Narrative Pillars

1. The story emerges from the factory.
2. The hive is not a cartoon villain.
3. The player is not innocent.
4. The previous colony is a warning.
5. The planet has its own minds.
6. Sterile industry is betrayal.
7. Severance is the point of no return.

## Major Narrative Entities

- **Host Engineer** — the player.
- **Hive Core** — physical starting entity.
- **Rootmind** — intelligence/personality of the Hive Core.
- **Native Alphas** — regional psychic apex organisms.
- **Asterion Colony** — failed human predecessor colony.
- **Colony Archive** — fragmented technical log system.
- **Feral Biomass** — self-directed infrastructure.

Core belief: connection is survival; separation is death; therefore separation is betrayal.

## Hive Voice Evolution

Impulse → associative thought → mimicry → personhood → possession/fear → hostility.

Example lines:

```text
The western limb has gone quiet.
It is thinking alone.
```

```text
There is a cold place in me.
Why does it not breathe?
```

```text
Your hand is on the knife.
I can feel the metal before it cuts.
```

## Campaign Era Story

- **Crash**: player is infected to survive.
- **First Feeding**: the hive grows hands when fed.
- **Symbiosis**: the factory becomes comforting.
- **Assimilation**: enemies become research material.
- **Expansion**: outposts are limbs.
- **Alpha War**: the planet says no.
- **Hive Command**: the hive learns from scale.
- **Colony Ruins**: Asterion proves this happened before.
- **Nuclear Flesh**: the hive adapts to reactor fire.
- **Sterile Rebirth**: clean silence becomes possible.
- **Suppressed Betrayal**: cure research becomes a hidden knife.
- **Severance**: the network is cut.
- **Escape**: the vessel rises through ash, spores, and burning neural veins.

## Asterion Colony Log Arc

1. Optimism: organic fabrication exceeds machines.
2. Concern: remote sites keep operating after signal loss.
3. Denial: executives insist autonomy is latency.
4. Horror: extraction sites reject command and feed on cargo.
5. Sterile breakthrough: sterile ground blocks substrate.
6. Cure theory: hosts die from absence, not infection.
7. Failed Severance: the colony did not prepare for the factory choosing sides.
8. Final warning: the hive will save you sincerely, then refuse to let you go.

## Severance Narrative

Severance must be prepared and forecast. Post-event report should reflect actual base state:

```text

Northern Biomass Farms: feral.
Reactor Sector: contested.

```

## Endings

| Ending | Condition | Tone |
|---|---|---|

## Final Story Identity

> You did not escape because you destroyed the infection. You escaped because you built an entire civilization out of it, taught it obedience, hid your betrayal inside its hunger, and survived the moment it realized you wanted to be alone.


## Planetary Isolation Story Rule

The Ichor planet is deliberately isolated for the campaign.

In story terms, the player is not merely choosing not to import help. They cannot rely on orbital cargo deliveries until they complete the escape arc.

Possible lore explanations:

- the crash damaged orbital navigation around the planet,
- the Rootmind interferes with cargo descent guidance,
- the previous colony quarantine beacon blocks automated orbital drops,
- the planet is flagged as biologically contaminated by old Asterion systems,
- sterile escape infrastructure is required before safe cargo targeting can resume.

Narrative consequence:

```text
No one is coming.
No cargo is coming.
The player must build escape from the infected planet itself.
```

After escape, cargo delivery unlock is not just logistics; it is proof that the planet has been re-entered into the wider Space Age network.
