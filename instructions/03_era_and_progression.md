# The Ichor Engine
## Era and Progression Document — Consolidated Version 0.2

## Purpose

This document defines the full campaign progression spine from crash to escape. Each era introduces a new factory problem, emotional beat, and production/research layer.

## Era Overview

| Era | Name | Core Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Crash | survive, salvage, first infection |
| 1 | First Feeding | feed hive, grow first substrate |
| 2 | Symbiosis | build stable biological starter factory |
| 3 | Assimilation | capture native traits and grow organs |
| 4 | Expansion | trains, outposts, Neural Veins |
| 5 | Alpha War | fight regional psychic bosses |
| 6 | Hive Command | megabase-scale biological control |
| 7 | Colony Ruins | recover failed human industry |
| 8 | Nuclear Flesh | uranium biology and reactor hearts |
| 9 | Sterile Rebirth | clean-room industry and contamination |
| 10 | Suppressed Betrayal | hide cure and Severance prep |
| 11 | Severance | factory civil war |
| 12 | Escape | final launch through hostile remains |

## Planetary Campaign Frame

The entire Era 0–12 progression occurs on the dedicated Ichor planet.

Canonical campaign state:

```text
Factorio target: 2.x
Expansion framework: Space Age planetary model
Ichor planet state before escape: quarantined / unreachable for cargo deliveries
Ichor planet state after escape: reintegrated / cargo delivery permitted
```

Era implication:

| Era | Space Age / Cargo State |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | stranded; no orbital cargo support |
| 9–10 | sterile industry is local and hidden, not imported |
| 11 | Severance happens before interplanetary reintegration |
| 12 | escape vessel completes the isolation arc |
| post-12 | cargo deliveries and wider Space Age integration may unlock |

This prevents outside logistics from bypassing the living-factory progression.


## Era 0 — Crash

Theme: survival and contamination. Goals: recover salvage, craft basic tools, scan Hive Core, harvest biomass, survive first native scavengers, create first substrate seed.

Buildings: Manual Analysis Bench, Wreckage Recycler, Primitive Biomass Pit, Hive Core, Crude Substrate Seed.

End condition: feed the wounded Hive Core enough raw biomass to stabilize the first Living Substrate patch.

## Era 1 — First Feeding

Theme: the infection becomes infrastructure. Goals: automate biomass, produce Nutrient Slurry, spread substrate, place first bio machines, process first ore, stabilize hunger.

Unlocks: Hive Hunger, Substrate Node, Growth Chamber I, Digestive Smelter I, Basic Tendril, Tendon Track, Neural Pylon I.

End condition: stable nutrient/ore production and first Synapse Cluster.

## Era 2 — Symbiosis

Theme: first real living factory. Goals: automate starter base, build defenses, research Biomass and Neural Science, manage loyalty warnings, begin mutation detection.

Unlocks: Neural Coherence, Building Loyalty, Spike Spitter, Chitin Wall, Growth Chamber II, Digestive Smelter II, Sensory Eye, Pheromone Gland.

End condition: unlock capture and Genome Analyzer.

## Era 3 — Assimilation

Theme: enemies become technologies. Goals: capture organisms, process specimens, sequence genomes, unlock trait branches, grow specialized organs, begin intentional mutation.

Unlocks: Spore Capture Capsule, Containment Sac, Genome Analyzer, Organ Harvester, Mutation Vat, Acid/Chitin/Wing/Venom/Sensory traits.

End condition: discover first Alpha field.

## Era 4 — Expansion

Theme: remote bases are living limbs. Goals: build Bio-Locomotive, run outpost trains, connect Neural Veins, defend rail/vein infrastructure, manage first serious isolation event.

Unlocks: Bio-Locomotive I, Cargo Carapace Wagon, Fluid Bladder Wagon, Neural Train Station, Train Synapse Anchor, Long-Range Neural Vein, Outpost Brood Governor, Stack Tendril, Arterial Duct.

End condition: reach resource/territory blocked by an Alpha.

## Era 5 — Alpha War

Theme: regional psychic warfare. Goals: scout Alpha territories, build dampeners, defend against interference, defeat/capture Alphas, harvest Alpha Organs.

Alpha examples: Shellmother, Acid Matriarch, Sky Brood Apex, Burrow Crown, Ember Maw, Frost Warden, Storm Leviathan, Glowback Sovereign.

End condition: first major Alpha organ upgrade; hive grows more ambitious.

## Era 6 — Hive Command

Theme: biological megabase control. Goals: scale production, automate feeding, build Brood Hives and drones, stabilize mutation, manage regional coherence, build advanced neural infrastructure.

Unlocks: Neural Pylon III, Synapse Relay, Brood Hive, Wing Drone, Construction Broodling, Endocrine Tower, Genetic Stabilizer, Mutation Scrubber, Neural Duct.

End condition: discover previous human colony ruins.

## Era 7 — Colony Ruins

Theme: humanity already failed here. Goals: explore ruins, recover archives, salvage old circuits, restore old machinery, learn clean-room theory, discover cure records.

Unlocks: Archive Decoder, Salvage Scanner, Ruin Extractor, Old-World Circuit, Refurbished Circuit, Containment Science, Failed Cure Records.

End condition: recover enough data for nuclear-biology and sterile containment paths.

## Era 8 — Nuclear Flesh

Theme: small suns grown inside organs. Goals: mine uranium, produce radioactive slurry, grow reactor hearts, manage cooling/radiation, unlock high power and dangerous weapons.

Unlocks: Uranium Digestor, Radioactive Slurry, Isotope-Seeking Tissue, Living Reactor Heart, Contained Reactor Heart, Radiation Scrubber, Reactor-Heart Locomotive, Bone-Lance Artillery.

End condition: enough power/materials for sterile industry.

## Era 9 — Sterile Rebirth

Theme: first clean silence. Goals: create Sterile Ground, build clean rooms, purify metals, build sterile circuits, prevent contamination, suppress Hive Awareness.

Unlocks: Sterile Ground, Clean-Room Assembler, Sterile Inserter, Sterile Conveyor, Clean Pipe, Purification Chamber, Contamination Scrubber, Sterile Lab.

End condition: first cure-stage technology and serious Hive Awareness pressure.

## Era 10 — Suppressed Betrayal

Theme: hiding the knife. Goals: build cure intermediates, expand sterile industry, construct suppression grid, reduce dependence on biological logistics, install Loyalist Anchors, prepare Severance.

Unlocks: Cure Lab, Retroviral Agent, Neural Antagonist, Final Cure Dose, Memory Loop Organ, False Hunger Chamber, Severance Core, Loyalist Anchor.

End condition: Severance Spire ready or forced awareness crisis.

## Era 11 — Severance

Theme: factory civil war. Activation effects: Hive Core becomes hostile, biological entities resolve loyalty, sterile sectors become prime targets, trains/drones split allegiance, feral zones surge, post-Severance research unlocks.

Goals: defend sterile sectors, stabilize cure, reclaim/purge key regions, restore logistics, contain feral zones.

End condition: stable post-Severance industry and final escape construction.

## Era 12 — Escape

Theme: clean engineering through a dying hive. Goals: build Escape Vessel Silo, produce final clean/hybrid components, defend launch, stabilize cure, launch.

Victory components: Sterile Structural Frames, Clean Rocket Control Units, Hybrid Launch Organs, Orbital Life-Support Capsules, Anti-Contamination Shielding, Navigation Core, Hybrid Fuel Chambers, Stabilized Cure.

## Progression Non-Negotiables

Player is infected early; hive saves player; player feeds and grows hive; substrate is core build surface; ore remains important; capture unlocks trait tech; outposts are risky living limbs; Alphas are psychic regional bosses; colony ruins unlock sterile path; nuclear flesh enables high power; sterile industry is hidden betrayal; Severance splits the factory; escape requires sterile and loyalist bio mastery.

## Final Progression Statement

The engineer survives by becoming part of an alien factory, masters it, learns it is learning, secretly rebuilds clean industry, cuts free, and escapes through the living civilization they created.
